# Is it a blue fucking wave?

Hour by hour: what to watch Tuesday night and how to tell if we're
winning. Every number is sourced (see the bottom): Cook, Decision Desk
HQ, Chuck Todd, Silver Bulletin and more. The attitude is all mine.
Times are Eastern, with Pacific below.

| Tag | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **HELL YES** | good for Dems |
| **FFS** | GOP beating expectations |
| **CHILL** | early count can lie |

---

## The House: 218 for a majority

Now R 218, D 214. Cook projects Dems at **227 to 237**. Silver Bulletin:
Dems about **95%** to win the House.

It's almost certainly ours. The real question is how big. See the ladder.

## The Senate: Dems need a net +4

VP Vance breaks ties. Hold MI, GA, NH, MN, then flip four of: **NC, OH,
ME, IA, TX, AK, KS, SC**. Independent Dan Osborn (NE) could scramble the
math.

Odds vary a lot: Silver ~79% D (Oct 30); Decision Desk HQ 57% and
VoteHub 60% (Oct 23).

## Rule #1: Early returns lie

Judge margins against expectations and 2024, not who's ahead. Counting
order can fake a lead either way. Nothing's real until Wasserman says
"I've seen enough."

A red state that won't get called? That's usually good news.

Spartz (R) v J.D. Ford (D) · Spartz clearly favored. Watch Hamilton

## 7:00 ET (4:00 PT)

Virginia, Georgia, South Carolina, most of Florida, Vermont, parts of NH.

*Normal midterm, or a 2018-style night?*

### Virginia ladder · Todd's small / medium / large test

**SMALL · VA-2** Kiggans (R) v Luria (D). Virginia Beach swing seat;
Luria favored.

- **HELL YES** Luria wins, as expected.
- **FFS** Kiggans holds: no wave. DDHQ: D majority odds fall below 50%.

**MEDIUM · VA-1** Wittman (R) v Taylor (D). Trump +5; Cook toss-up.

- **HELL YES** Taylor wins: the classic wave test; Todd says ~235 seats.
- **CHILL** Wittman won by ~13 in 2024, so even close is good.

**LARGE · VA-5** McGuire (R) v Perriello (D). Trump +12.

- **HELL YES** Perriello wins: tsunami territory, Todd says ~250.
- **CHILL** VA-7 (Vindman, D) should be a routine hold.

### Florida · the fast-count state

80%+ of ballots expected counted by 8:00 ET. Gov: Donalds (R) v Jolly
(D). Senate: Moody (R) v Nixon (D). GOP redrew House lines.

- **HELL YES** Governor still uncalled at 9:00 (Todd: "a very good
  sign"). FL-14 Castor and FL-25 Moskowitz both hold: near-certain House
  majority (DDHQ).
- **FFS** GOP wins statewide by 5+. Castor and Moskowitz both lose: D
  majority odds about 1 in 3.

Stretch seats: FL-22 (open, toss-up), FL-9 Soto (Trump +16 new lines),
FL-27 Salazar (Latino backlash test).

### Georgia · Senate and Governor

Senate: Ossoff (D) v Mike Collins (R), Cook Likely D. Gov: Jackson (R) v
Bottoms (D).

- **HELL YES** Ossoff margin bigger than expected: good omen for the
  Southeast.
- **FFS** Jackson called by 11:00 means GOP turnout beat expectations;
  Todd says that rules out a wave.
- **CHILL** Metro Atlanta counts last. Early R leads mean little. Under
  50% = runoff.

### South Carolina · the sleeper

Senate: Darline Graham (R, appointed) v Annie Andrews (D). Cook and
Sabato: Likely R. Silver avg R +1.7.

- **HELL YES** Strong Black turnout, which surged in the primaries
  (Todd). Race uncalled late.
- **FFS** Called early for Graham.

Tsunami House tells: SC-1 (open) and SC-2 (Wilson, Trump +14).

### Kansas · Senate

Hamilton (D) v Marshall (R) · Cook toss-up · Silver avg D +1.1 (RCP had

### Pennsylvania House · four GOP seats to defend

Also at 8:00: NJ-7 Kean (Cook Lean D), MI-4 Huizenga (toss-up), MO-2
